Before your Google Business Profile can show up in the map pack, local search results, or Google Maps, it has to be verified. Verification is Google's way of confirming that the business is real, that it operates where it says it operates, and that the person managing the profile actually has the right to do so.

For most small businesses in the Rio Grande Valley, verification takes anywhere from a few minutes to a few weeks depending on which method Google assigns. Getting it right the first time matters — a failed or stuck verification can delay your local visibility by weeks, and some errors require contacting Google support to resolve.

## Why does Google Business Profile verification matter for local rankings?

An unverified Google Business Profile is completely invisible. You can fill out every field, add photos, list all your services — and none of it will appear in local search results until verification is complete. Verification also unlocks your ability to respond to reviews, post updates, access performance insights, and appear in Google Maps.

For a service-area business in the RGV trying to rank in multiple cities, getting verified quickly and correctly is step one of everything else.

## How do I create or claim a Google Business Profile?

Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account you want to use to manage the business. Search for your business name. If it already exists in Google's system — common for any business that's been operating a few years — you'll see it in the results and can claim it. If it doesn't exist, create it from scratch.

Fill out all required fields completely before starting verification: business name exactly as it appears on your signage or license (no keyword additions), primary category, address or service area, phone number, and website. The more complete your profile is before verification, the smoother the review process.

## What are the Google Business Profile verification methods in 2026?

Google offers several methods depending on your business type, location, and history. The method they assign varies — you don't always get to choose.

**Video verification** is now the default for most new profiles. You record a short unedited video (30 to 90 seconds) showing your business location, signage, and workspace or vehicle. The video must be one continuous take — no cuts, no captions, no music. Submit through the Google Business Profile mobile app.

**Phone or text verification** sends a code to the phone number on your profile. Fastest method when available — completes in minutes.

**Email verification** sends a code to an email associated with the business. Less common but straightforward.

**Postcard verification** mails a physical code to your business address. Takes 5 to 14 days. Less common since video verification became the default, but still used for certain business types. The code expires in 30 days.

**Live video call** is available when video verification has been rejected. A Google representative joins a call and you show your location, equipment, or signage in real time.

## How long does Google Business Profile verification take?

Timeline depends on the method Google assigns. Phone, text, and email verification typically complete within minutes to an hour. Video verification takes 3 to 7 business days for Google to review the submission. Postcard verification takes up to 14 days for delivery, then is instant upon code entry. A live video call verifies immediately upon completion.

Do not make significant changes to your profile — especially business name, address, or primary category — while verification is pending. Changes can restart the entire review process.

## Why does Google Business Profile video verification get rejected?

Video rejection is the most common point of failure. Google's review system looks for specific signals, and missing any of them typically results in rejection without detailed explanation.

**The video has any gaps, cuts, or pauses.** The recording must be one continuous take from start to finish.

**Business signage isn't clearly visible.** Brick-and-mortar locations need their sign on camera. Service-area businesses need branded truck or vehicle wrap visible.

**The address doesn't match the profile.** For physical locations, the street number should be visible on the building or mailbox. For service-area businesses, a photo ID showing your address is typically required.

**The business looks residential with no commercial indicators.** Show professional equipment, branded materials, or a work vehicle. Home-based businesses need commercial evidence even if operating from a residential address.

If your video is rejected, resubmit immediately with a new recording — slower narration, better lighting, every required element shown clearly. Most businesses succeed on the second or third attempt.

## How does GBP verification work for service-area businesses?

Service-area businesses — HVAC companies, electricians, roofers, plumbers — don't serve customers at a physical location. For verification, you'll set service areas instead of (or in addition to) an address. Your video needs to show: your branded vehicle, professional equipment or tools, and something establishing your identity as the business owner (business card, uniform, or ID in frame).

One important note: Google still uses your listed address (even if hidden from the public) to calculate your proximity to searchers. A service-area business operating in McAllen with an address in Harlingen may have difficulty ranking in McAllen's map pack for competitive searches — proximity is a real factor in map pack eligibility.

## What should I do immediately after my Google Business Profile is verified?

The first 30 days post-verification are when Google is actively indexing your profile signals. Use them.

Complete every profile field: hours, attributes (women-owned, bilingual, veteran-owned — whatever applies), services, products if applicable. Upload at least 5 to 10 photos — your logo, team, work, and vehicle or location. Write a complete 750-character business description that includes your primary service, your city, and your service area. Post your first GBP update — a brief introduction post.

A profile that goes from zero to 10 reviews in the first 30 days sends a strong signal that the business is active and legitimate. That early momentum accelerates your ranking timeline meaningfully.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does Google Business Profile verification take?**
It depends on the method. Phone and email verification can complete in minutes. Video verification typically takes 3 to 7 business days. Postcard verification takes up to 14 days for delivery plus code entry. Live video calls verify immediately.

**My video verification was rejected. What do I do?**
Resubmit with a new video — fully continuous, signage or branded vehicle clearly visible, address numbers in frame, recorded in good lighting. Most rejections are fixable by addressing those specific gaps on the second attempt.

**Can I verify a Google Business Profile for a home-based business?**
Yes. The address stays hidden from the public, but you need a verification video that establishes commercial activity from that location. Show professional equipment, branded materials, or a work vehicle at the address.

**What happens if I let a pending verification expire?**
Your profile won't appear in Google Search or Maps until verified. If you don't complete verification within the allowed window, you'll need to start the process over.

**Can I change my business information after verification?**
Yes, but significant changes — business name, address, primary category — may trigger re-verification. Google monitors for profile changes that could indicate a different business.

**I'm claiming an existing listing and it's asking me to verify. Is that normal?**
Yes. Even when claiming a listing Google created from public data, you still need to verify that you're the legitimate owner. The process is the same as a new profile.
