## 4. SERP Intelligence

### Title Tag Patterns That Rank

From top 5 results across all 6 seed keywords:

| Pattern | Frequency | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| "[Service] [City]" (bare) | 6/30 | "SEO McAllen" (cobaltdigital) |
| "[City] [Service]" | 5/30 | "McAllen SEO Company \| Expert Local SEO Services..." |
| "[Adjective] [City] [Service]" | 4/30 | "Expert McAllen SEO, Search Engine Optimization" |
| "[Service] \| [Brand]" | 3/30 | "Local SEO Services McAllen TX - Antares Systems" |
| "[Superlative] [Service] [City]" | 4/30 | "The Best SEO Company in McAllen" |

**Key pattern insight:** Most competitors use bare or superlative patterns ("Best," "Expert," "Top"). Zero competitors are using the BENEFIT in their title tags ("Get to the Top 3 Map Pack," "99+ PageSpeed"). This is RankRGV's differentiator opportunity — lead with the outcome, not the service name.

**Recommendation:** Use `[Primary Keyword] | [Specific Outcome] | RankRGV` — the benefit segment is what no competitor is doing, and it directly addresses the searcher's intent.

### Content Gap Analysis

**Topics competitors cover (must match):**
- Dedicated McAllen SEO service page — imagineitstudios, cobaltdigital, rgvseoservice
- Google Maps optimization mention — most competitors
- Local SEO process/steps — most service pages
- Service area coverage listing — most competitors

**Topics NO competitor covers (blue ocean opportunities):**
1. **Business automation as a distinct service** — Cobalt has a "business consulting" page but zero automation-specific content. No local agency has automation + lead capture pages. This entire category is wide open.
2. **Bilingual search strategy / RGV-specific market dynamics** — No competitor addresses the Spanish/English dual market, border economy seasonality, or unique buying behavior of RGV consumers.
3. **Missed call text-back / AI response automation** — Zero local agency pages for this. National SaaS companies (HummingAgent.ai) are the only ones showing up.
4. **Real-time dashboard / reporting transparency** — No competitor markets transparency as a differentiator. Everyone says "results" — nobody shows *how* they report.
5. **Service Area Business SEO strategy** — For local businesses without a storefront (home services, consultants), zero content exists in this market about GBP optimization for SABs.
6. **Industry-specific landing pages** — Healthcare SEO McAllen, legal SEO McAllen, contractor SEO McAllen — none of the local competitors have industry-specific pages.
