What&#39;s the difference between local SEO and regular SEO?

Regular (organic) SEO targets people searching for information anywhere. Local SEO specifically targets people searching for businesses in a specific city or area — &#39;plumber in McAllen,&#39; &#39;dentist near me,&#39; &#39;SEO company McAllen TX.&#39; Local SEO also includes Google Maps optimization, which is the top 3 results shown on a map above the regular search results. For most RGV businesses, local SEO is what actually drives phone calls and customers.

Do I need local SEO if I already have a Google Business Profile?

Yes — having a Google Business Profile is just the starting line. Most GBP profiles in the RGV are incomplete, unoptimized, and missing the signals Google uses to rank you. Local SEO covers optimizing your GBP, building your website to rank for city-specific searches, earning local citations and reviews, and creating service-area content. Your GBP is one piece; local SEO is the whole system.

What is the Google Map Pack and how do I get in it?

The Google Map Pack is the section at the top of local search results showing a map and three business listings. It appears for nearly every service-based search (&#39;SEO company McAllen,&#39; &#39;restaurant near me&#39;). It gets roughly 3× the clicks of the organic results below it. Getting into the Map Pack requires a fully optimized Google Business Profile, consistent local citations, positive reviews, and a website Google trusts as a local authority. That&#39;s exactly what we build.

Can SEO help my business show up in both McAllen and surrounding cities?

Absolutely — and this is one of the biggest advantages of the approach we use. We build dedicated pages for every city you serve. So if you serve McAllen, Edinburg, and Mission, you get three separate ranking pages, each targeting local searches for that city. This is how service area businesses (ones without a physical storefront in every city) dominate multiple markets simultaneously.
