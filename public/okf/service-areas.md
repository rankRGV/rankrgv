## Questions about our RGV service areas

Which cities in the Rio Grande Valley does RankRGV serve?

RankRGV serves businesses throughout the Rio Grande Valley, with dedicated SEO strategies for McAllen, Brownsville, Edinburg, Harlingen, Mission, and Pharr. Each city gets its own set of optimized pages — not a copy-paste of another city&#39;s content. We understand the local market dynamics, search patterns, and competitive landscape for each RGV city.

Does RankRGV work with businesses that serve multiple RGV cities?

Yes — this is one of the most common scenarios we handle. Many RGV businesses operate as service area businesses (SABs): they serve customers across multiple cities but don&#39;t have a physical location in each one. We build the right page architecture for every city you want to rank in — dedicated city pages, proper schema markup, and a GBP service area configuration that covers your full territory without diluting your primary market.

Do you build separate pages for each RGV city?

Yes, and this is critical. A single &quot;we serve the whole RGV&quot; page does not rank for city-specific searches. Google needs to see a dedicated page for &quot;SEO company Brownsville TX&quot; to rank you for that search — and a different page for Edinburg, Harlingen, and so on. We build each city page with unique, locally-relevant content so they rank independently and don&#39;t compete with each other.

Can you rank my business in cities where I have no physical presence?

Yes. Google&#39;s local algorithm allows service area businesses to rank in cities they actively serve, without a physical address there. The key is the right combination: a properly configured Google Business Profile with service areas set, dedicated city pages on your website, and local signals (citations, reviews mentioning the city) that confirm you actually operate there. We handle all of this.

Is SEO competition the same across all RGV cities?

No — and this matters for prioritization. McAllen is the most competitive market in the RGV, though still weak compared to Austin or Dallas. Brownsville, Edinburg, and Harlingen are slightly less competitive. Mission and Pharr have the least competition for most keywords. We prioritize your primary city first, then build outward across the RGV as rankings establish.
