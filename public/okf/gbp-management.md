At minimum, post weekly. Google rewards active profiles in local rankings, and businesses that post regularly get measurably more profile views, clicks, and calls than businesses that don&#39;t. If your business is new or unclaimed on Google, full GBP setup includes claiming the listing, verifying ownership, choosing the right primary and secondary categories, defining your service area, writing the business description, uploading initial photos, setting hours and contact details, and configuring services and products.
