## Paid ads questions from RGV business owners

How much should I spend on Google Ads in McAllen?

For most McAllen service businesses, the right starting range is usually $1,000 to $3,000 per month in ad spend. Enough to gather real data, see which searches convert, and build momentum without lighting money on fire. The exact number depends on your category, your margin, and how aggressive you want to be. We start with the search demand and cost-per-click in your market, then back into a budget that makes business sense.

What&#39;s the difference between Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Google Ads captures existing demand. Someone is already searching for a roofer, attorney, HVAC company, or med spa and wants a solution now. Meta Ads creates demand and stays in front of people based on interests, behavior, and retargeting audiences. Google is usually the best first move for high-intent service leads. Meta becomes powerful when you want to stay visible, build demand, and retarget the traffic you&#39;re already generating.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

No, not on standard small-business accounts. We use a flat monthly management fee so our incentives stay clean. If you increase budget, you should do it because the numbers justify it, not because your agency makes more when you spend more. For larger accounts over roughly $10,000 per month in ad spend, we usually scope custom pricing.

How long until I see results from Google Ads?

Paid search moves much faster than SEO. Most accounts start generating usable data within the first week and meaningful lead patterns within the first 30 days. That does not mean day-one perfection. The first month is where we learn which keywords, audiences, ads, and landing pages are actually producing calls and form fills. After that, optimization gets sharper quickly.

Can I run ads and SEO at the same time?

Yes, and for many RGV businesses that&#39;s the smartest setup. Ads generate leads now while SEO compounds over time. Paid search covers the short-term demand you need this month; SEO builds the long-term rankings that reduce your dependence on paid traffic later. Together, they create a much more stable acquisition system.

Do you write the ads or do I?

We handle the ad copy structure, testing plan, and revisions. Your input still matters because you know your offers, objections, and best customers better than anyone. The strongest campaigns combine your real sales language with our performance-testing process.
